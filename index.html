Brand story

Because some things deserve to exist only once.

Nilouh Story was born from a shared love for art, textiles and timeless beauty. Created by three women—a daughter, mother and aunt—the brand grew from quiet afternoons of painting, draping fabrics, admiring delicate craftsmanship and believing that clothing should feel personal.

Inspired by the softness of the blue water lily, Nilouh Story brings together hand-painted artistry, luxurious fabrics and thoughtful craftsmanship to create pieces that feel rare and deeply individual.
