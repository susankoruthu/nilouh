Brand story

Because some things deserve to exist only once.

Nilouh Story was born from a shared love for art, textiles and timeless beauty. Created by a daughter, mother and aunt, it grew from quiet afternoons of painting and draping fabrics.

Inspired by the softness of the blue water lily, each piece brings together hand-painted artistry, beautiful fabric and thoughtful craftsmanship.

Helpful details

Before you
enquire.

Are all Nilouh pieces one of a kind?+

Yes. Since every motif is painted by hand, small variations are part of each piece’s individuality.

How do I place an order?+

Tap any Enquire button to message us on Instagram. We will share availability, sizing and order details with you personally.

Can I request a custom piece?+

Yes. Tell us your occasion, preferred garment and inspiration through Instagram, and we will let you know what is possible.

How should I care for hand-painted fabric?+

Hand wash separately in cold water, dry in shade and iron inside out to care for the fabric and painted artwork.
